Questions

Frequently asked questions about Maratonia

What people ask before installing it: what you need, what the watch does on its own, and where the runs end up.

Can I run without carrying my iPhone?

Yes. The plan travels to the Apple Watch before the session, and from there the watch guides the whole run on its own: intervals, target pace and voice prompts through Bluetooth headphones. It does not need signal or the phone nearby.

Which training plans does it include?

Five starting points: first 5K in 6 weeks, toward 10K in 8, improve your 5K in 8, half marathon in 12 and marathon in 16. Every plan is editable block by block, so you can change intervals, paces and days before sending the session to the watch.

How does it guide pace while I run?

The watch compares your pace against the target for the current block and announces intervals, kilometer splits and corrections out loud. It uses smoothed pace instead of the raw GPS reading, so normal signal noise does not send you chasing a number that is not real.

Where are my runs saved?

In Apple Health. Distance, heart rate, calories and the GPS route are recorded as a workout on your device, so you can review them from Maratonia, from Health or from Fitness. The training data stays yours.

What do I need to use Maratonia?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Bluetooth headphones are optional and only needed if you want to hear the voice prompts and music while the phone stays at home.

Does it work if I have never run before?

That is what the first 5K plan is for. It alternates walking and easy running over six weeks so you build consistency before worrying about pace, and the watch tells you when to change from one to the other.
